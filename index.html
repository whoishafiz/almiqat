About us

Taking Brunei's jemaah to the Haramain

Al-Miqat Travel & Tours Sdn Bhd is a Brunei travel company. We run Umrah packages from Bandar Seri Begawan to Madinah and Makkah, and we host visitors on tours of Brunei.

A miqat is the boundary where a pilgrim enters ihram and begins Umrah. We chose the name because that is the moment we work towards. We take care of the flights, visas, hotels, trains and paperwork, so your attention can stay on worship.

Every Umrah group travels with a mutawwif and our own escort staff, from check-in at Brunei International Airport to your return home.

Transit planning

How to use a long layover in Brunei before Umrah

What to check before you leave the airport, and how much time you really need.

Read the guide

Check your entry requirements first. Visa rules depend on your passport, so confirm with the airline or Brunei Immigration before you travel.

Leave enough time. Send us your arrival and departure times on WhatsApp and we will tell you whether your layover fits our Umrah Transit tour.

If you are already in ihram, let us know. We will plan the stops and the meal with that in mind.

Mosque guide

Visiting Brunei's two great mosques

Omar Ali Saifuddien and Jame' Asr Hassanil Bolkiah, and what makes each one special.

Read the guide

Omar Ali Saifuddien Mosque stands beside a man-made lagoon in the heart of the capital. Its golden dome is lit at night, which is why it features on our transit and night tours.

Jame' Asr Hassanil Bolkiah Mosque in Kiarong is the country's largest. Its gardens and domes are best seen in daylight.

Dress modestly, remove your shoes at the entrance and keep voices low near the prayer hall. Your guide will tell you about prayer times and any visiting restrictions on the day.

Umrah preparation

Packing for Umrah in Ramadan

What to bring for Madinah and Makkah, and what we give you.

Read the guide

Every Al-Miqat Umrah package comes with a gift set. The package page lists what is in it.

Bring comfortable walking shoes, light clothes, your ihram or prayer garments, personal medicine and a refillable water bottle. Fasting days are long and the walks to the Haramain add up.

Check the baggage allowance on your package before you shop. Leave room for dates and Zamzam water on the way home.
